profiles/maeda_pb1_spec.dat — Maeda linear articulatory model (LAM) specification for speaker PB
(semi-polar grid, alpha-beta sagittal-to-area coefficients, lip/tongue/larynx factor loadings, wall contour).

Source: https://github.com/sensein/VocalTractModels (vtcalcs/data/specdata/pb1_spec.dat)

The model and data are due to Shinji Maeda:
  S. Maeda (1990), "Compensatory articulation during speech: evidence from the analysis and synthesis of
  vocal-tract shapes using an articulatory model", in Speech Production and Speech Modelling (Kluwer), 131-149.
src/formant_ml/engine/articulation.py is a differentiable re-implementation of vtcalcs/src/lam_lib.c
(lam, sagittal_to_area, semi_polar, convert_scale) from the same repository.
